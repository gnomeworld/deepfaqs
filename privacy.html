Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

DeepFaqs is an independent, fan-made FAQ website for Creatures of the Deep. We aim to collect as little personal information as possible.

1. Who to contact

For privacy questions, access or deletion requests, contact the DeepFaqs site operator at fishyloni@gmail.com.

2. Information we collect

You can browse DeepFaqs without an account or giving us your name or email address. If you choose to use our Google Forms “Submit a Question” form, you may submit a question, a suggested category, and an optional player name. The form is configured not to collect email addresses. Please do not include sensitive or other personal information in your question.

3. Why we use submissions

We review submissions to improve the FAQ and may edit questions for clarity before publishing them. We do not intend to publish player names or identifying details without permission. Where applicable, we rely on legitimate interests in operating and improving this community resource, subject to your privacy rights.

4. How long submissions are kept

We aim to delete original form submissions within 60 days of submission, whether or not they have been reviewed. This includes copies in the linked response spreadsheet. Edited, non-identifying FAQ content already published on DeepFaqs may remain on the site. Removal from the form and spreadsheet does not necessarily erase backups or records retained by service providers under their own policies.

5. Services and technical information

DeepFaqs is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical information, such as IP addresses and request logs, when serving the website. The question form is hosted by Google Forms; Google processes form submissions and may process technical information in accordance with its policies. These services may process information in other countries.

See GitHub's privacy statement and Google's privacy policy.

6. Cookies and analytics

DeepFaqs does not intentionally use advertising trackers or analytics cookies. A check of the homepage on October 14, 2026 found no browser storage and no third-party network requests during page load. This check does not cover the separate Google Form or external sites, which may use their own technologies. We will review this notice if the site changes.

7. External links

Our site links to third-party resources, including game guides, community sites, Discord, and Google Forms. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies.

8. Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live and the applicable law, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, or objection to processing of your personal information, and to complain to a relevant data protection authority. Email fishyloni@gmail.com with enough detail to identify your submission. We will respond as required by applicable law.

9. Updates

We may update this policy when DeepFaqs changes. The updated policy and date will appear on this page.

DeepFaqs is a fan-made community resource and is not affiliated with Infinite Dreams.
